Architecture review on the field findings (s-004): three firmware versions
across three stations, ninety-minute reflashes per display. Theo proposed the
edge-gateway hub — flash once per station, displays pull from the gateway —
over keeping per-display autonomy with a better flashing ritual. The room
agreed, and d-008 landed the same day. Bram takes gateway provisioning.
