Theo and Dev spent the day in the field at Ashport Central and Ferry Quay:
after the last firmware update, the three pilot stations drifted to three
firmware versions, and a board that lost power would not recover its clock.
Reflashing takes roughly ninety minutes per display carried to the platform.
Per-display autonomy (d-003) is failing on maintenance, not on rendering.
