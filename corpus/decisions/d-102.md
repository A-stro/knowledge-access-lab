Context: platform displays need live vehicle positions with sub-second
perceived freshness; HTTP polling burns battery and lags.

Options considered: HTTP polling against a feed API; MQTT 5.0 with retained
messages and shared subscriptions.

Decision: the Perchboard display fleet speaks MQTT 5.0 to a transit-side
broker; retained messages let a display recover its last-known state after
reconnect, and shared subscriptions fan out cleanly once relays arrive.
Informed by mqtt-5-notes.

Consequences: one broker to operate; session expiry and reason codes make
reconnects observable.
