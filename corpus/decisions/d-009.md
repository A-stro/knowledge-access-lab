Context: Ashport Central's platform is sunlit most of the day; the first
palette washed out in daylight.

Options considered: the original blue-on-dark palette; green-and-amber after
Odessa's contrast testing at the platform (s-008) and the WCAG contrast
notes.

Decision: Wayfare's displays render in green-and-amber — green for live
departures, amber for alerts and delays — both testing well above the
contrast floor in daylight.

Consequences: renderer theming is a config swap; June owns the design tokens.
