Context: Ridgeway's operations desk needs ad-hoc joins across feeder sensor
feeds and Fathom weather-adjusted forecasts.

Options considered: a time-series server for raw telemetry; ClickHouse over
MergeTree partitions.

Decision: Cinderboard lands feeder data in ClickHouse and queries it with
plain SQL — the wallboard needs aggregate scans more than a retention
engine, informed by clickhouse-notes.

Consequences: no separate database server to operate beyond the ClickHouse
process; ingestion stays batch-oriented.
