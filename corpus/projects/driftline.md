Driftline is a small ferry-schedule print service for Bayline Transit: the
day's timetables rendered to thermal printers at three ticket counters
(decision d-116: print from Bayline's timetable feed, not a local copy).
Built in a two-week gap by Pavel Sorokin and Nils Aasen between the
Perchboard ship and the Ridgeway restart; wired in s-139 and shipped
2026-05-25 after Amara's checklist run (s-140).
