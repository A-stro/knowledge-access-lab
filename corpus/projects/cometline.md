Cometline is an exhibit kiosk for Millhaven Observatory: an offline-capable
kiosk browser (decision d-106) playing the Night Sky over Vantage Bay
exhibit loop with captions, wall-mounted at the observatory entrance. It
survived a 48-hour offline soak during hardening (s-129) and opened to
visitors 2026-05-02; the opening review with Ida Kovacs is m-115.

Team: Pavel Sorokin (kiosk), Gwen Torvald (exhibit design), Amara Diallo
(QA).
