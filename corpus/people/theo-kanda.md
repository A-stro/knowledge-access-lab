Theo Kanda co-founded Meridian Studio with Mara Voss and is its systems
engineer. He proposed the edge-gateway hub that reversed Wayfare's display
architecture, works Beacon Grid's ingest, and championed the Kestrel CRM
experiment until its cancellation.
