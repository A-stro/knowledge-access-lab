Context: Bellweather's archive scans number in the tens of thousands of
pages; OCR throughput sets the digitization schedule.

Options considered: local OCR on the studio's machines; a remote OCR API.

Decision: route Larkspur OCR through a remote API — roughly four times local
throughput at an absorbable per-page cost, informed by the ocr-comparison
benchmark notes.

Consequences: the digitization schedule holds; throughput is assumed to scale
linearly with scan volume. Later contradicted by the latency benchmark in
s-006, which found archival page scans far below the assumed rate.
