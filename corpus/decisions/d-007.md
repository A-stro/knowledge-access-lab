Context: Greenline sensor arrays publish at short intervals; REST polling on a
timer would hammer their gateways.

Options considered: REST polling on a timer; MQTT subscriptions to sensor
topics.

Decision: Beacon Grid ingests via MQTT subscriptions, reusing the same
protocol notes that shaped Wayfare's transport (mqtt-311-notes, also behind
d-002).

Consequences: one consumption pattern across two projects; sensor gateways
stay quiet.
