Architecture review on the field findings (s-109): two firmware versions
across three stations, seventy-minute reflashes per display, clock loss
after power cuts. Yusuf proposed the edge relay per station — flash once per
station, displays pull from the relay — over keeping per-display autonomy
with a better flashing ritual. The room agreed, and d-109 landed the same
day. Sofia takes relay provisioning.
