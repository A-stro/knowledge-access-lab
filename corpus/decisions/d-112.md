Context: Granite Pier's deck is sunlit most of the day; the first
teal-on-slate palette washed out in daylight.

Options considered: the original teal-on-slate palette; high-contrast
white-on-black after Elif's legibility testing at the pier (s-116) and the
signage legibility notes.

Decision: Perchboard's displays render white-on-black — white for live
departures, an inverted panel for alerts and delays — both reading cleanly
at distance in daylight.

Consequences: renderer theming is a config swap; Gwen owns the design
tokens.
