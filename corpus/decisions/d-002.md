Context: platform displays need live vehicle positions with sub-second
perceived freshness; HTTP polling burns battery and lags.

Options considered: HTTP polling against a feed API; MQTT with retained
messages and QoS 1.

Decision: the Wayfare display fleet speaks MQTT 3.1.1 to a transit-side
broker; retained messages let a display recover its last-known state after
reconnect. Informed by mqtt-311-notes.

Consequences: one broker to operate at Halcyon; occasional QoS-1 duplicate
renders, accepted for freshness.
