Context: Ridgeway feeder sensors publish at short intervals; REST polling on
a timer would hammer their gateways.

Options considered: REST polling on a timer; MQTT 5.0 shared subscriptions
to feeder topics.

Decision: Cinderboard ingests via MQTT 5.0 shared subscriptions, reusing the
protocol notes that shaped Perchboard's transport (mqtt-5-notes, also behind
d-102).

Consequences: one consumption pattern across two projects; feeder gateways
stay quiet.
