Context: the observatory entrance has unreliable wifi and no attendant; the
kiosk must play its loop through outages.

Options considered: a networked web page; a kiosk-mode browser with the full
exhibit loop cached.

Decision: Cometline runs a kiosk-mode browser with the exhibit loop cached
offline, a watchdog restart, and an idle attract cycle, informed by the
kiosk browser notes.

Consequences: the loop updates by scheduled refresh windows; a dead router
is invisible to visitors.
