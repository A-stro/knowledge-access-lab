Odessa Klein is Meridian Studio's user researcher. She ran rider research at
Ashport Central for Wayfare — watching how passengers actually read departure
boards — and drove the palette contrast testing with June Park that settled
the green-and-amber palette. She also runs archive-user interviews for the
Larkspur project.
