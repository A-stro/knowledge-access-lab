Context: each platform display needs local rendering; per-display autonomy
avoids putting a central server in the pier buildings.

Options considered: per-display autonomy with single-board computers; a
station hub coordinating its displays.

Decision: each Perchboard display runs its own single-board computer with
local fallback rendering and no central station server — autonomy per
display, informed by the panel field research (signage-panel-notes).

Consequences: wiring stays simple; firmware updates must be flashed board by
board, and field clocks drift after power cuts. Superseded by d-109 after
field maintenance pain (s-109, m-107).
