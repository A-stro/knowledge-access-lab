Petra Lindqvist is Halcyon Transit's service planning lead and the client-side
sponsor of the Wayfare program. She set the pilot scope at the kickoff — three
stations, live vehicle positions, outdoor readability, a June ship — and
stayed the client voice through the architecture change and the ship.
