office. Scope agreed: platform displays for three pilot stations — Ashport
Central, Ferry Quay, Millbrook Bridge — live vehicle positions, sub-second
perceived freshness, and outdoor readability. Petra sponsors; the ship target
is June. Transport and architecture decisions followed within days (d-002,
d-003).
