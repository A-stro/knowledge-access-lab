The pricing discussion weighed hourly billing assembled from raw timesheets
against flat monthly billing per client: hourly recovers every scrap of
effort but costs Lorna a reconciliation day each month and reads
unpredictably to clients; flat monthly is one predictable number per client
with timesheets kept internal for capacity planning. Flat monthly won, and
d-005 landed three days later.
