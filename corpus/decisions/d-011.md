Context: Bellweather wants its digitized collections viewable and portable; a
custom gallery would lock scans to one interface.

Options considered: a custom-built gallery; an IIIF-based viewer over
standardized image and manifest APIs.

Decision: Larkspur ships an IIIF-based viewer — deep zoom, page order, and
metadata from standardized manifests — so the scans stay portable across
viewer implementations, informed by iiif-overview.

Consequences: viewer work is integration rather than invention; prototyped
in s-010 and shown at the Bellweather review (m-007).
