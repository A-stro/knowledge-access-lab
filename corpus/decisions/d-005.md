Context: the studio had been assembling hourly invoices from raw timesheets;
reconciliation cost Lorna a day per month.

Options considered: hourly billing from raw timesheets; flat monthly billing
per client.

Decision: Ledgerline moves to flat monthly billing per client, agreed in the
pricing discussion (m-003) — clients see one predictable number; timesheets
stay internal for capacity planning.

Consequences: invoicing is a one-step monthly run; scope creep now surfaces
in planning, not on the invoice.
