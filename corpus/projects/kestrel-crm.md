Kestrel was Theo Kanda's proposal for an internal CRM experiment — a
lightweight tracker for studio client relationships — announced at the March
all-hands (m-004). Kestrel started 2026-03-31, the day after the all-hands;
Theo prototyped contact pages and Lorna Vey ran a two-week ops trial.

Cancelled 2026-04-21 (decision d-010): with the ops trial load falling on
Lorna alone, no one could own it. Data export and teardown happened in s-009.
