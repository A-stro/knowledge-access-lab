Context: ticket-counter schedules change with the season; a maintained local
timetable copy goes stale the first week.

Options considered: a maintained local timetable file; rendering from
Bayline's timetable feed.

Decision: Driftline renders the day's timetables from Bayline's feed at
print time, informed by ferry-timetable-notes — counters can print a fresh
proof sheet whenever service changes.

Consequences: counters depend on the feed being up; the runbook covers the
offline fallback sheet.
