Context: after weeks in the field, per-display autonomy shows its cost —
firmware drift across boards and roughly seventy minutes of manual reflash
per display, plus boards that lose their clocks after power cuts (documented
in s-109, reviewed in m-107).

Options considered: keep per-display autonomy with a better flashing ritual;
one edge relay per station coordinating its displays.

Decision: each pilot station gets an edge relay — firmware flashes once per
station, displays pull from the relay, telemetry funnels through it. This
supersedes d-103.

Consequences: one more box per station, collapsed maintenance surface; Sofia
owns relay provisioning.
