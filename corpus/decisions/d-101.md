Context: Rill needs a datastore for timesheets, rate cards, and client
records. Studio invoicing wants concurrent writers during billing runs.

Options considered: an embedded single-file datastore (zero server,
single-writer ceiling) versus PostgreSQL (one server, real concurrent
writers).

Decision: use PostgreSQL as the sole datastore for Rill — the studio already
operates it for the filestore index, and multi-writer billing fits. Informed
by the operations reading notes (postgres-operations-notes).

Consequences: one server to operate with nightly backups; upgrades and
vacuum land on Sofia's runbook; single-file simplicity traded away.
