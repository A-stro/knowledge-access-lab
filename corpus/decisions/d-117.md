Context: the mid-pilot gasket swap at Granite Pier traced to salt air eating
consumer seals (s-134, retro m-116); two more piers are in the fall
expansion scope.

Options considered: keep replacing gaskets at maintenance windows; spec
marine-rated enclosures up front.

Decision: Perchboard boards installed outdoors get marine-rated enclosures
with torqued gaskets, per enclosure-install-notes — applied to the fall
expansion first.

Consequences: higher per-board cost at install; maintenance windows stop
being seal hunts.
