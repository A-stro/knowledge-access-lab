Context: archival titles repeat across collections; filenames by title
collide and lose scan order.

Options considered: titles as filenames; box-and-leaf identifiers matching
the physical archive.

Decision: scans are named by box and leaf — box seven, leaf fourteen —
matching the physical order Aurelio's team shelves, informed by the archival
handling notes.

Consequences: scan order is recoverable from names alone; the gallery's
anchor URLs (d-118) inherit stable identifiers.
