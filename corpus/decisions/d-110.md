Context: boards that lost power would not recover their clocks; departure
boards showed wrong times until someone climbed the pier with a laptop
(s-109).

Options considered: manual clock sets during maintenance windows; NTP time
sync via chrony with the relay as the source.

Decision: every Perchboard board syncs to its station relay's clock with
chrony, with drift alarms wired to the depot dashboard, informed by
chrony-notes.

Consequences: no more manual clock sets; a drifted board now pages the depot
instead of lying to passengers.
