Context: each platform display needs local rendering; per-display autonomy
avoids putting a central server in stations.

Options considered: per-display autonomy with single-board computers; a
station hub coordinating its displays.

Decision: each Wayfare display runs its own single-board computer with local
fallback rendering and no central station server — autonomy per display,
informed by the single-board field notes (embedded-single-board-notes).

Consequences: wiring stays simple; firmware updates must be flashed board by
board. Superseded by d-008 after field maintenance pain (s-004, m-005).
