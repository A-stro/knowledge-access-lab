Copperleaf Studio is an eleven-person software design studio in Vantage Bay,
founded in 2019 by Ines Marchetti and Yusuf Adeyemi. It builds operational
software for civic and cultural clients — transit, museums, publishers,
utilities — plus a small line of internal tools it runs for itself. The
studio works out of the Wharfworks Collective, a converted cannery on the
waterfront.
