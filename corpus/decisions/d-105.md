Context: the studio had assembled flat quotes by hand; scope creep landed in
Nils's lap at invoice time.

Options considered: flat monthly billing per client; hourly billing with
per-project rates from a rate card.

Decision: Rill bills hourly with per-project rates, agreed in the billing
discussion (m-104) — clients who buy sprints see the effort their scope
consumes; timesheets stay the source of truth.

Consequences: invoicing is an export from tracked time; rate cards need
effective periods (landed as d-115).
