Retro after shipping Wayfare on 2026-06-12. What worked: the edge-gateway
reversal (d-008) collapsed the maintenance surface — flashing is now a
station visit, not a fleet archaeology dig; Odessa's platform contrast
testing made the green-and-amber palette readable in full sun; MQTT retained
messages recovered cleanly through network drops. What hurt: QoS 1 delivered
duplicate renders during reconnects — next time, dedupe on the display, not
in ops. Katrin's QA signoff checklist is adopted as the studio default for
client ships.
